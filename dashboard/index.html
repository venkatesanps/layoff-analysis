About this data

  • TWC publishes one spreadsheet per year. The files overlap (the 2024 file repeats late-2023 notices), so 232 exact duplicate rows were removed, plus 2 "Updated" notices that re-filed the same headcount.
  • Other amended notices stay in, so a few layoffs may be counted twice. Notices list planned layoffs; final numbers can be lower.
  • Organization names are cleaned from the job site name: legal suffixes, site names and "dba" parts are removed, and about 150 well-known employers are matched by hand.
  • WARN covers employers with 100 or more workers and mass layoffs or closures, so small layoffs do not appear.
